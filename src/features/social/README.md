# 🥣 Social Feature (formerly Posts)

Welcome to the Social feature! This is your go-to example for building a modern social feed with posts, comments, likes, and more—now with less postmeal and more convention.

## What You'll Find Here
- **PostCard**: Displays a single post (formerly PostCard)
- **FeedGrid**: Shows a grid of posts (formerly FeedGrid)
- **SavedPosts**: User's saved posts (formerly SavedPosts)
- **CommentList**: List of comments for a post (formerly CommentList)
- **PostForm**: Create or edit a post (formerly PostForm)
- **CommentForm**: Add a comment
- **Hooks & Services**: For fetching, creating, updating, and deleting posts/comments
- **Tests & Storybook**: All with a touch of Spwoo's character in the comments

## Spwoo's Tip
> "Social features are like a good breakfast—best when shared! Use these examples to build your own feed, and don't forget to sprinkle in your own flavor."
